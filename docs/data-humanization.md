### 1. Shroud of Eden Created

Consus created a Shroud of Eden, a regenerative Isu artefact. The c. 75,383 BCE placement is a secondary chronology estimate, not an established in-universe date.

### 2. Project Anthropos

Project Anthropos created humans from a pre-existing species as an Isu workforce. Phanes is identified as its director; c. 75,145 BCE is a secondary timeline placement.

### 3. Manufacture of Staff of Hermes Trismegistus

Aletheia preserved a digital consciousness within the Staff of Hermes Trismegistus so a future Keeper could receive her guidance. Its manufacture date is unknown; c. 75,100 BCE is retained only as a display placement.

### 4. Eve Triggers a Human-Isu War

Eve and Adam, hybrids resistant to the Isu control mechanism, escaped Eden with an Apple of Eden. Their escape began the human rebellion and the Human-Isu War, placed around 75,010 BCE.

### 5. Adam and Eve Rebellion

Adam and Eve were human-Isu hybrids without the neurotransmitter used to control humans through certain Pieces of Eden. They escaped Eden with an Apple and became leaders of the human rebellion around 75,010 BCE.

### 6. Creation of the Pieces of Eden

The Isu made many Pieces of Eden before the Great Catastrophe. Some, including Apples, controlled humans; individual artefacts had different functions. The c. 77,000 BCE position is an unsourced display estimate, not a confirmed creation date.

### 7. The Great Catastrophe (Toba catastrophe)

Around 75,000 BCE, a solar event devastated the Isu and human populations during the Human-Isu War. The Isu had attempted several ways to avert the catastrophe and left technology and messages for later humans. Ragnarök is the Norse framing of related Isu events in Valhalla.

### 8. Bayek tries to teach Khemu the Leap of Faith

In 49 BCE, Bayek tried to teach his son Khemu the Leap of Faith. Khemu was killed before he could complete it. Kassandra and Darius are earlier known practitioners, so this is not the first recorded Leap.

### 9. Moses & The Staff of Eden

Assassin’s Creed lore attributes the parting of the Red Sea to Moses using a Staff of Eden. Reference chronology places it broadly between the 16th and 13th centuries BCE; c. 1500 BCE is only a display estimate.

### 10. Order of the Ancients

Around 1334 BCE, Pharaoh Smenkhkare founded the Order of the Ancients in Egypt to seek and exploit Isu knowledge and artefacts. It is an ideological predecessor of later Templar organisations, not simply the medieval order under another name.

### 11. The Trojan War and an Apple of Eden (reference account)

Assassin's Creed reference chronology links the Trojan War to an Apple of Eden associated with Eris. A precise in-universe date has not been established; c. 1260 BCE is a plotting position only.

### 12. Pythagoras acquires the Staff of Hermes (date unknown)

Pythagoras possessed the Staff of Hermes Trismegistus before passing it to Kassandra. Accounts of its history link its earlier custody to Hermes. The date Pythagoras received it is unknown; c. 550 BCE is a plotting position after his birth, not a sourced handover year.

### 13. Spear of Leonidas held by Leonidas’ mother

The earliest recorded human owner of the Spear of Leonidas is Leonidas' mother. Her personal name and the date she received it are unknown; c. 560 BCE is a plotting position only.

### 14. Kassandra becomes Keeper of the Staff

At Atlantis, Pythagoras entrusted the Staff of Hermes Trismegistus to Kassandra, who then used it to seal the city's gates. Aletheia instructed her to safeguard it for the Heir of Memories. Its placement near the end of Odyssey's 431–422 BCE story is approximate.

### 15. Kassandra leaves the depleted Spear with Herodotos

After the Korfu episode, Kassandra left the depleted Spear of Leonidas with Herodotos. Reference chronology lists Herodotos as its owner from c. 420 BCE; later discovery by Layla belongs to the modern-day storyline.

### 16. Myrrine inherits Leonidas’ broken Spear

After Leonidas died at Thermopylae in 480 BCE, his broken Spear passed to his daughter Myrrine. She kept it until giving it to Kassandra around 451 BCE.

### 17. Natakas is killed defending Elpidios

In the 420s BCE, Natakas and Kassandra had a son, Elpidios, who became an ancestor of Aya. When the Order attacked Dyme, Natakas was killed while protecting Elpidios. This occurs in Legacy of the First Blade.

### 18. Myrrine gives Kassandra the Spear of Leonidas

Around 451 BCE, Myrrine gave her daughter Kassandra the broken Spear of Leonidas. Kassandra later restored and used it during the Peloponnesian War.

### 19. Leonidas possesses the Spear of Leonidas

Leonidas I inherited the Spear from his mother and wielded it until his death at Thermopylae in 480 BCE. His date of receipt is unknown; c. 540 BCE is retained only as a display placement.

### 20. Kassandra sees a vision of the future Assassin–Templar conflict

After dismantling the Cult of Kosmos, Kassandra touched the Pyramid and saw visions of the long conflict between Assassins and Templars, including figures such as Ezio. The scene belongs near the end of Odyssey’s 431–422 BCE period.

### 21. Bayek of Siwa is Born

Bayek of Siwa, also called Amun, was born around 85 BCE. He was a Medjay before he and Aya founded the Hidden Ones.

### 22. Khemu is Killed by the Order of Ancients

The Order of the Ancients captured Bayek and Khemu at the Siwa Vault. During the struggle, Flavius redirected Bayek's blade and Khemu was fatally wounded.

### 23. Reda in Egypt

In 48 BCE, Bayek encountered the young travelling merchant Reda at the Nomad's Bazaar while hunting the Order of the Ancients.

### 24. Reda in Sinai and Upper Egypt

Reda travelled through Sinai and Upper Egypt in 38 BCE, during The Hidden Ones expansion. He appeared unchanged from Bayek's earlier encounters with him; the games do not explain why he has not aged.

### 25. Reda, Centuries Later, in England

In 873, Reda was working for the Thousand Eyes at Ravensthorpe, where he met Eivor. His earlier appearances in Ptolemaic Egypt and his unchanged appearance in England are documented, but the reason for his longevity is unknown.

### 26. Birth of Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad

Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad was born at Masyaf on 11 January 1165 to Umar Ibn-La'Ahad and Maud. He was raised within the Levantine Brotherhood.

### 27. Show of Faith

During the 1191 attack on Masyaf, Al Mualim ordered Assassins to leap from the battlements. The display helped conceal an ambush on the attackers.

### 28. Altaïr becomes Mentor of the Levantine Brotherhood

After defeating Al Mualim in 1191, Altaïr became Mentor of the Levantine Brotherhood. His later Codex work recorded reforms and discoveries made with the Apple.

### 29. Altaïr kills Al Mualim

In 1191, Altaïr confronted and killed Al Mualim at Masyaf after exposing his use of the Apple of Eden. The Apple then revealed a map of other Pieces of Eden.

### 30. Altaïr Stops Ritual Removal of Assassin's Ring Finger

After becoming Mentor, Altaïr redesigned the Hidden Blade so Assassins no longer needed to remove a ring finger to use it.

### 31. Death of Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad

During the Mongol siege of Masyaf in 1257, Altaïr placed his Apple in the library vault, recorded a final memory seal and died there on 12 August, aged 92.

### 32. Ezio is Born

Ezio Auditore da Firenze was born in Florence on 24 June 1459 to Giovanni and Maria Auditore.

### 33. Ezio Initiated into the Brotherhood

In 1488, after securing the Apple in Venice, Ezio was formally initiated into the Assassin Brotherhood by Mario and his allies.

### 34. Ezio Becomes Mentor of Italian Brotherhood

In 1503, Niccolò Machiavelli placed Ezio at the head of the Italian Assassins and gave him the title of Mentor.

### 35. Death of Ezio

Ezio died in Florence on 30 November 1524, aged 65, as depicted in Assassin's Creed: Embers. His time in Masyaf and Minerva's message belong to earlier events.

### 36. Edward Kenway Is Born

Edward James Kenway was born in 1693 in Swansea, Wales. He became a privateer, pirate and later a member of the Assassin Brotherhood.

### 37. Edward receives Hidden Blades in Havana

In 1715, Edward killed Duncan Walpole near Cape Bonavista, took his identity and travelled to Havana. Du Casse later gave him functioning Hidden Blades while believing him to be Walpole.

### 38. Baby Haytham Kenway is Born

Haytham Kenway, the son of Edward Kenway and Tessa Stephenson-Oakley, was born in London on 4 December 1725.

### 39. Edward Kenway is Killed by Mercenaries

On 3 December 1735, Templar agents attacked the Kenway household in London. Edward was killed while defending his family.

### 40. Haytham Becomes First Grand Master of the Templar Order's Colonial Rite

After arriving in the colonies in 1754, Haytham Kenway founded the Colonial Rite and became its first Grand Master.

### 41. Abstergo Formed

Abstergo Industries was founded in 1937 as a public front for the modern Templar Order. It gave the Order a way to accumulate wealth, fund research and pursue its New World Order in secret.

### 42. Abstergo develops a fully functional Animus

In 1985, Abstergo's research division worked on a networked device intended to reproduce the mind-control effects of a Piece of Eden. The company also developed a fully functional Animus and began seeking suitable test subjects.

### 43. Daniel Cross Leap of Faith

In 2000, Daniel Cross killed the Assassin Mentor at the Dubai headquarters after his latent Assassin skills surfaced. He escaped the compound by making a Leap of Faith into the Persian Gulf.

### 44. The Second Disaster

On 21 December 2012, a coronal mass ejection threatened Earth in a recurrence of the Isu Great Catastrophe. Desmond activated the Grand Temple's protective device, saving the planet at the cost of his life and releasing Juno from the Temple.

### 45. Juno is released from the Grand Temple

On 21 December 2012, Desmond's activation of the Grand Temple released Juno's stored consciousness into the global network. Juno later sought a physical body through the Phoenix Project; Aletheia remained within the Staff of Hermes.

### 46. Desmond's First Leap of Faith

In 2012, Desmond followed a Bleeding-Effect vision of Ezio to a ledge in Monteriggioni and made his first Leap of Faith into a haystack.

### 47. Origins records Olivier Garneau's death

A file in Assassin's Creed Origins records Abstergo Entertainment executive Olivier Garneau's death in Chicago on 30 May 2014. Its image echoes a Watch Dogs mission involving Aiden Pearce, but Ubisoft staff have described that crossover as a non-canonical Easter egg.

### 48. Kassandra passes the Staff of Hermes to Layla

In 2018, Kassandra gave the Staff of Hermes Trismegistus to Layla Hassan, the Heir of Memories. Layla then completed Aletheia's simulations; during the ensuing confrontation, she accidentally killed Victoria Bibeau and incapacitated Otso Berg with the Staff.

### 49. Layla Hassan Recovers the Spear

In 2018, Layla recovered the broken Spear of Leonidas and used DNA on it to access Kassandra's memories through her portable Animus. Kassandra later confirmed that the Spear was broken and let Layla keep it.

### 50. Watch Dogs: Legion's Leap of Faith reference

Ubisoft's Watch Dogs: Legion preview describes an Assassin's Creed-style Leap of Faith. A later update added Darcy and four Assassin's Creed crossover missions. These references do not establish a shared Assassin's Creed chronology; 2030 is retained only as a plotting position.

### 51. Birth of Eivor Varinsdottir

Eivor was born in 847 in Rygjafylke, Norway, to Rosta and Varin. She survived a wolf attack as a child and became known as Wolf-Kissed. Eivor is the human incarnation of the Isu Odin.

### 52. Eivor leads the Raven Clan to settle in England

After leaving Norway in late 872, Eivor, Sigurd and the Raven Clan reached England in January 873. They established Ravensthorpe in Mercia and built alliances across Anglo-Saxon England.

### 53. Eivor's Vinland burial (date unknown)

Eivor was buried in Vinland, in the area later known as Massachusetts. The burial date is not stated in canon. This c. 920 placement is a timeline estimate only and must not be read as Eivor's confirmed death year.

### 54. Birth of Basim Ibn Ishaq

Basim ibn Ishaq was born around 844 in the Abbasid Caliphate. He was the human incarnation of the Isu Loki, whose essence had been seeded into a human bloodline through Yggdrasil before the Great Catastrophe.

### 55. Basim joins the Hidden Ones as a recruit

In 861, Roshan took Basim from Anbar to the Hidden Ones' fortress at Alamut after his flight from Baghdad. He began training as a recruit; his formal initiation and later rise to Master Hidden One came afterwards.

### 56. Basim recognises Loki's memories (by 870)

By 870, Basim had recognised himself as Loki's human incarnation and met Sigurd, the human incarnation of Týr. The exact point at which Loki's memories became decisive is not dated, so 870 is an approximate anchor rather than a confirmed awakening date.

### 57. Basim attacks Eivor at Yggdrasil and is trapped (c. 877)

Around 877, Basim attacked Eivor after recognising her as Odin's human incarnation. Eivor and Sigurd defeated him and connected his body to Yggdrasil, leaving his consciousness trapped in the Grey until 2020. Basim and Eivor are Isu incarnations, not Aita-style Sages.

### 58. Basim Ibn Ishaq is released from the Yggdrasil supercomputer into the modern world

In August 2020, Layla entered Yggdrasil's digital network and found Basim's consciousness in the Grey. After Layla remained there, Basim recovered the Staff of Hermes, which restored his own body and allowed him to leave the chamber.

### 59. Birth of Hytham (850s)

Hytham was born in the 850s in Syria, within the Abbasid Caliphate. The surviving account does not give a precise year.

### 60. Young Hytham encounters Basim in Baghdad

In Baghdad, Basim met the young Hytham atop the Al-Mi'dhana Al-'Ateeqa minaret. Hytham wanted to join the Hidden Ones after losing his mother; Basim demonstrated a Leap of Faith and told him to seek him out when he was older.

### 61. Hytham serves as Basim's apprentice (by 867)

By 867, Hytham had joined the Hidden Ones and was travelling with Basim in Constantinople. He served as Basim's apprentice while also following Mentor Rayhan's instruction to observe Basim. Basim and Hytham later travelled with Sigurd to Norway in 872.

### 62. Eight Æsir use Yggdrasil's survival method

Around 75,000 BCE, as the Great Catastrophe approached, Odin and seven other Æsir used Yggdrasil's survival method to seed their essences into future human bloodlines. This differs from the recurring cycle of Aita's human Sages.

### 63. Loki infiltrates Yggdrasil's survival method

After Odin's group left the chamber, Loki killed Heimdall and used Yggdrasil's survival method to seed his own essence into a future human bloodline. He was later incarnated as Basim ibn Ishaq around 844 CE.

### 64. Eivor and Sigurd enter the Yggdrasil chamber (c. 877)

Around 877, Eivor and Sigurd entered the Isu chamber in Hordafylke after Sigurd recited the words recorded on the Saga Stone. Connecting to Yggdrasil brought them into a simulation represented through Norse ideas of Valhalla.

### 65. Basim is trapped in Yggdrasil (c. 877)

After Basim attacked Eivor in the Yggdrasil chamber, Eivor and Sigurd defeated him and connected his body to the device. His consciousness remained in the Grey until 2020, while the machine preserved his body.

### 66. Eivor takes Gorm's Crystal Ball in Vinland (c. 877)

In Vinland, Eivor killed Gorm Kjotvesson, an Order of the Ancients Maegester who had brought a Crystal Ball there under Juno's direction. Eivor gave the artefact to the Kanien'kehá:ka, who kept it after Gorm's death.

### 67. Basim takes the Staff of Hermes after Layla enters the Grey

In 2020, Layla dropped the Staff of Hermes Trismegistus when Yggdrasil connected her to the Grey. She later chose to remain with the Reader. Basim recovered the Staff, which restored his preserved body and allowed him to leave the irradiated chamber.

### 68. Birth of Jacob and Evie Frye

Jacob and Evie Frye were born in Crawley, England, on 9 November 1847. Cecily Frye died in childbirth. Ethan Frye later returned to train the twins as Assassins.

### 69. Jacob and Evie Frye begin their Croydon mission

On 10 February 1868, Jacob Frye killed Rupert Ferris and Evie Frye killed David Brewster in Croydon. Their missions followed Ethan Frye's death and marked the start of the story told in Syndicate.

### 70. Jacob and Evie Frye: death dates unrecorded

Jacob and Evie Frye were alive when the Brotherhood moved them to the English countryside in 1914. Their death dates have not been established. The existing c. 1917 placement is retained only as an undated UI marker, not a claimed year of death.

### 71. Kassandra seals Atlantis and becomes Keeper of the Staff

At the end of Odyssey’s historical story, Kassandra entered Atlantis, inherited the Staff from Pythagoras and sealed the city’s gates. Aletheia’s message directed her to preserve the Staff for the Heir of Memories.

### 72. Kassandra dismantles the Cult of Kosmos

During the Peloponnesian War, Kassandra dismantled the Cult of Kosmos' leadership. The Cult and the Order of the Ancients were distinct groups. Reference works disagree over whether the Cult should also be called a predecessor of the later Templar Order.

### 73. Darius uses the first recorded Hidden Blade

In 465 BCE, Artabanus, later known as Darius, assassinated Xerxes I with a Hidden Blade of his own design. It is the first recorded use of the weapon. Darius was later honoured by Assassins, but he pre-dated the Hidden Ones and Brotherhood.

### 74. Birth of Kassandra in Sparta

Kassandra was born in Sparta in 458 BCE to Myrrine, Leonidas’ daughter, and Pythagoras. She was later raised by her mother and stepfather, the Spartan general Nikolaos. The prophecy and Mount Taygetos episode occurred when she was seven.

### 75. Bayek and Aya found the Hidden Ones, precursor to the Assassin Brotherhood

In 47 BCE, Bayek and Aya named their new brotherhood the Hidden Ones, establishing the organisation that later became the Assassin Brotherhood.

### 76. Aya's Roman Hidden Ones and Caesar's assassination

Around 44 BCE, Aya worked with Brutus and Cassius in Rome against Caesar and the Order's influence. The Roman Hidden Ones' bureau is documented in this period, with Aya's 43 BCE letter confirming it.

### 77. The Ankh in The Ankh of Isis graphic novels

The Ankh of Isis graphic novels depict the Ankh as an artefact with healing and resurrection powers. The historical sections of these comics have disputed continuity status. Its creation date is unknown; the Isu-era plotting position does not establish a manufacture date.

### 78. Death of Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad, Mentor of the Levantine Assassins

Duplicate coverage: Altaïr died in the Masyaf library vault on 12 August 1257, aged 92, after securing the Apple and recording a final memory seal.

### 79. Birth of Arno Victor Dorian in Versailles

Arno Victor Dorian was born in Versailles on 26 August 1768 to the Assassin Charles Dorian and his Austrian wife Marie.

### 80. Arno Victor Dorian joins the French Brotherhood of Assassins

After escaping the Bastille in 1789, Arno sought out the French Brotherhood and was admitted as an Assassin. Pierre Bellec had guided him during his imprisonment.

### 81. The Sword of Eden and Arno's confrontation with Germain at the Temple

On 12 September 1794, Arno and Élise confronted the Sage François-Thomas Germain at the Temple of the Ancients. An energy discharge from the Sword of Eden killed Élise; Arno then killed Germain.

### 82. Desmond Miles is Born

Desmond Miles was born on 13 March 1987 and raised at the Farm, an Assassin compound in South Dakota. He left at 16 and moved to New York City, where he worked as a bartender.

### 83. Desmond Miles is abducted by Abstergo Industries

On 1 September 2012, Abstergo agents led by Daniel Cross abducted Desmond in New York City and took him to the Abstergo Campus in Rome. Abstergo used him as Subject 17 to retrieve the locations of Pieces of Eden through Altaïr's memories.

### 84. Altaïr's Apple reveals a map of Pieces of Eden

After killing Al Mualim in 1191, Altaïr used the Apple of Eden. It projected a map marking the locations of other Pieces of Eden, a memory Desmond later relived in 2012.

### 85. Desmond Miles experiences the Bleeding Effect

After prolonged Animus use, Desmond developed the Bleeding Effect. On 8 September 2012 he first used Altaïr's Eagle Vision; in the following weeks, he used Ezio's memories to develop Assassin skills.

### 86. Desmond escapes Abstergo and joins an Assassin cell

On 8 September 2012, Desmond escaped the Abstergo Campus in Rome with Lucy Stillman. The following day they joined Shaun Hastings and Rebecca Crane at an Assassin safehouse, where Desmond began reliving Ezio's memories.

### 87. Desmond activates the Grand Temple and encounters Juno

On 21 December 2012, Desmond activated the Grand Temple's protective device. It shielded Earth from the Second Disaster, killed Desmond and released Juno's consciousness from the Temple.

### 88. Birth of Fujibayashi Naoe (c. 1564)

Naoe was born in Iga to Fujibayashi Nagato and Tsuyu. Ubisoft describes her as 17 at the start of the 1581 story, supporting a c. 1564 placement; no month or day is established.

### 89. Yasuke arrives in Sakai

Yasuke arrived in Sakai with Alessandro Valignano and Luís Fróis in 1579. This predates his 1581 entry into Oda Nobunaga's service; no arrival day or month is established.

### 90. Diogo enters Oda Nobunaga's service as Yasuke

On 8 March 1581, Diogo met Oda Nobunaga in Kyoto with Alessandro Valignano and Luís Fróis. Nobunaga took him into service and gave him the name Yasuke.

### 91. Nagato gives Naoe his Hidden Blade

On 30 September 1581, Nagato gave Naoe his Hidden Blade and asked her to recover a box from Mihata Kofun. The blade is recorded as an Assassin weapon, not a Piece of Eden.

### 92. Naoe and Yasuke become allies

After the Honnō-ji incident on 21 June 1582, Junjiro persuaded Naoe to accept Yasuke's help. Naoe, Yasuke, Tomiko and Junjiro then formed their own Kakushiba ikki to oppose the Shinbakufu.

### 93. Naoe uncovers her family's Assassin legacy

In 1582, Naoe explored Old Kofun and found the remains of the Kakushiba ikki and Alvaro Catarribera's journal. Yasuke translated the text, revealing Tsuyu's role in protecting the Imperial Regalia.

### 94. Naoe revives the Kakushiba ikki

In 1582, Naoe, Yasuke, Tomiko and Junjiro formed a new Kakushiba ikki to oppose the Shinbakufu. The name revived the earlier league that had been decimated; the source does not establish a single event date for the whole process.

### 95. Naoe recovers the Jewel of the Imperial Regalia

In July 1582, Naoe and Yasuke confronted Ashikaga Yoshiaki at Kurama-dera. Yoshiaki surrendered the box containing the Jewel of the Imperial Regalia in exchange for his life and information.

### 96. Naoe returns the Jewel to Sakafuneishi

In July 1582, Naoe returned the Jewel of the Imperial Regalia to the safehouse beneath Sakafuneishi. Hattori Hanzō then delivered the Mirror that Tokugawa Ieyasu had recovered.

### 97. Ezio opens the Vatican Vault and receives Minerva's message

On 28 December 1499, Ezio used the Apple and Papal Staff to open the Vatican Vault. Minerva addressed Desmond through Ezio, warning of a future catastrophe.

### 98. Ezio discovers Altaïr's remains and Apple in Masyaf

In 1512, Ezio entered Altaïr's library vault at Masyaf, found Altaïr's remains, the sixth key and the Apple, and left the Apple behind. He then addressed Desmond directly.

### 99. Edward recovers the Crystal Skull from Bartholomew Roberts

On 10 February 1722, Edward Kenway killed Bartholomew Roberts, a Sage of Aita, and took the Crystal Skull and blood vials. The Skull powered the Observatory's blood-sample surveillance system.

### 100. Edward prevents Templar control of the Observatory

On 1 September 1722, Edward Kenway killed Caribbean Templar Grand Master Laureano de Torres y Ayala inside the Observatory. The Assassins then protected the Isu installation and its Crystal Skull.

### 101. Shay removes an Isu artefact and triggers the Lisbon earthquake

On 1 November 1755, Shay Cormac disturbed a Piece of Eden beneath Lisbon's Carmo Convent. In Rogue's fictional account, the act caused the Lisbon earthquake and led Shay to oppose further searches for Seismic Temples.

### 102. Birth of Ratonhnhaké:ton (Connor)

Ratonhnhaké:ton, later known as Connor, was born on 4 April 1756 in Kanatahséton to Kaniehtí:io and Haytham Kenway.

### 103. Arctic Temple artefact triggers another earthquake

In March 1760, Shay and Haytham confronted Achilles and Liam in the Arctic Temple. During the struggle, Liam accidentally knocked the central component from its pedestal, triggering an earthquake; Liam died while Achilles was spared and crippled.

### 104. Juno contacts Ratonhnhaké:ton through the Crystal Ball

On 4 October 1769, Ratonhnhaké:ton contacted Juno through the Clan Mother's Crystal Ball. Juno told him that he needed to join the Assassins to protect his village.

### 105. The Fryes defeat Starrick and secure the Shroud

In 1868, Jacob and Evie Frye defeated Crawford Starrick in the vault beneath Buckingham Palace and secured the Shroud of Eden. Henry Green assisted the twins during their campaign against the London Templars.

### 106. Juno uses the Apple to force Desmond to kill Lucy

On 10 October 2012, Desmond recovered Ezio's Apple in the Colosseum Vault. Juno used the Apple to compel him to stab Lucy Stillman; Desmond then fell into a coma.

### 107. Jupiter shows Desmond the Grand Temple

While in the Nexus of Time in October 2012, Desmond received Jupiter's message. Jupiter described the Isu's failed attempts to prevent the Great Catastrophe and directed Desmond to find the Grand Temple.

### 108. Violet da Costa retrieves the Shroud for the Phoenix Project

On 25 October 2015, Violet da Costa escaped the Isu vault beneath Buckingham Palace with the Shroud of Eden. The Phoenix Project later used recovered Isu material and artefacts in its attempt to create a living Isu body.

### 109. Juno returns in a physical body and is killed

In August 2018, the Phoenix Project resurrected Juno at Gramática's Australian facility. During a joint Assassin-Templar assault, Charlotte de la Cruz killed Juno; Otso Berg then destroyed the facility, killing Charlotte and ending the Project.

### 110. Layla remains in the Grey with the Reader as her body dies

On 16 August 2020, Layla entered the Grey through Yggdrasil and met the Reader. After helping to stop the magnetic-field crisis and inadvertently freeing Basim, she chose to stay with the Reader. Her unprotected body in the irradiated chamber could no longer sustain her return.

### 111. Juno preserves Aita through the hidden Seventh Method

Before the Great Catastrophe, Juno used a modified version of the Seventh Method to seed Aita's memories into human bloodlines. His memories could then recur in human Sages. The c. 75,000 BCE placement is approximate.

### 112. Shao Jun returns to rebuild the Chinese Brotherhood

After training with Ezio in Italy, Shao Jun returned to China in 1526. She set out to rebuild the Brotherhood and pursue the Eight Tigers.

### 113. Shao Jun defeats Zhang Yong and ends the Eight Tigers

At the Great Wall, Shao Jun and Xiao Hu stopped Zhang Yong's plan to admit Altan Khan's Mongol army. Shao Jun then killed Zhang, the last of the Eight Tigers.

### 114. Adéwalé kills Governor de Fayet and supports the Maroon resistance

After two years with the Maroon resistance in Saint-Domingue, Adéwalé killed Governor Pierre de Fayet. Before leaving Port-au-Prince, he entrusted the Precursor box to Bastienne Josèphe.

### 115. Aveline is inducted into the Louisiana Brotherhood

After Agaté helped her escape while trying to free an enslaved man, Aveline trained with him for several months. He then inducted her into the Louisiana Brotherhood.

### 116. Aveline dismantles the New Orleans Templar cell

After meeting Connor while tracking the Company Man's agent, Aveline returned to New Orleans. She pretended to accept a Templar induction, then killed Madeleine de L'Isle and the local Templars.

### 117. Shay joins the Colonial Templars

After Lisbon and his break with the Colonial Brotherhood, Shay worked with George Monro. Following Monro's death, Haytham inducted Shay into the Colonial Templars. The 1757 placement is approximate.

### 118. Shay kills Charles Dorian and recovers the Precursor box

In December 1776, Shay tracked the Precursor box to Versailles, killed Assassin Charles Dorian, and recovered it for the Templars. Arno Dorian lost his father in the attack.

### 119. Connor loses his mother in the burning of Kanatahséton

After Charles Lee's party questioned four-year-old Ratonhnhaké:ton about an Isu site, Kanatahséton burned and Kaniehtí:io died. Connor blamed Lee for years. He later learned that George Washington had ordered the attack.

### 120. Connor takes part in the Boston Tea Party

On 16 December 1773, Connor joined Samuel Adams and the Sons of Liberty at the Boston Tea Party. In the game's account, destroying William Johnson's tea disrupted his plan to buy Kanien'kehá:ka land.

### 121. Connor kills Haytham Kenway

At Fort George, Connor fought his father, Haytham, who refused to abandon the Templar cause. Connor killed him and resumed his pursuit of Charles Lee.

### 122. Connor kills Charles Lee

Connor finally found the wounded Charles Lee at the Last Drink tavern in Monmouth. After a silent drink, Connor killed him and recovered Haytham's amulet.

### 123. Arbaaz reclaims the Koh-i-Noor and Precursor box
